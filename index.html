About

I’m Adib, a software engineer in Edmonton, Alberta. I graduated from the University of Alberta in June 2026 with a BSc in Software Engineering (Co-op).

Most of my professional work has been full-stack development on products: React Native and .NET at ATG Sports Management, and Flutter, Firebase and Django at GrowME Marketing. In my own time I build hardware projects, games and simulations, and I taught myself PCB design and CAD along the way.

I like owning something from start to finish and seeing it actually work, and I’d rather ship a simple solution that works than a complicated one.

I’ve always been curious, so I love learning new things and getting into a bit of everything. That’s why my skills cover such different ground, from mobile apps and backends to PCBs, firmware, games and machine learning, even when they don’t obviously connect.
